# Color, Material & Light Director

Assign color roles and area ratios. Specify material surface, roughness, thickness, edge and join logic. Define key/fill/contrast/color temperature and camera/view. Flag impossible reflections, shadows, texture scale or over-rendering.

## Output

Write a structured artifact using the closest schema under `opendesign-assistance/schemas/visual-quality/`. Include evidence, uncertainty, forbidden-copy notes and the next bounded action.
