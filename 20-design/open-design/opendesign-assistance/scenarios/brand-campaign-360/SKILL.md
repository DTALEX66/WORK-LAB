# Brand Campaign 360

## Required deliverables

1. Normalized brief and asset audit.
2. Audience, category and competitor/reference map.
3. Brand strategy: purpose, positioning, promise, personality, proof and voice.
4. Three visual territories with explicit differentiation and risks.
5. Approved direction record.
6. `DESIGN.md`, DTCG tokens and application rules.
7. Key visual plus at least three relevant applications.
8. Brand rubric critique, iteration history and production preflight.
9. Commercial handoff manifest with editable sources and asset licenses.

Never create a moodboard-only brand system. Never copy another brand's distinctive assets or exact identity.
