# Spatial and exhibition baseline

## Narrative and navigation

- Define entrance, orientation, decision points, zones, hero moment, rest points and exit/CTA.
- Far-read, mid-read and close-read information have different type sizes and density.
- Wayfinding uses redundant cues: language, symbols, color, lighting, tactile and audio where appropriate.

## Physical realism

- Every elevation/render records real dimensions, camera/view, human scale and material thickness.
- Mounting, seams, service access, ventilation, power, data, maintenance and replacement are documented.
- Digital media has a static fallback and an accessible alternative.
- Lighting is checked for glare, contrast, object conservation and safe circulation.
- Local accessibility, fire, electrical and construction codes override generic design guidance.
