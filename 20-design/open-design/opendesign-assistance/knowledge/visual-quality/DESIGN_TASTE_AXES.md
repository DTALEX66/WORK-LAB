# Design Taste Axes

Taste is modeled as informed discrimination: the ability to see which decision strengthens the intended effect and why.

## Judgment pairs

- clear vs simplistic
- rich vs cluttered
- restrained vs empty
- expressive vs arbitrary
- luxurious vs decorative
- experimental vs unusable
- realistic vs over-rendered
- cultural vs stereotyped
- minimal vs generic
- systematic vs monotonous

Every critique must identify the pair currently out of balance, cite visible evidence and propose one bounded correction.
