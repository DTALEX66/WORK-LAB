# Master Method Use Policy

The project studies masters to improve judgment, not to manufacture imitations.

## Allowed

- compare multiple works and periods
- identify transferable decisions, constraints, process and quality signals
- map a designer to broader lineages and historical context
- translate observations into anonymous composition, type, color, material, image and production rules
- use names in research reports and citations

## Prohibited

- final prompts asking for a living or historical designer's exact style
- copying iconic layouts, logos, proprietary typefaces, pictograms, illustrations, photographs, garments or buildings
- training or packaging protected archive images without permission
- combining names as a superficial “style cocktail”
- claiming a method card is the designer's own statement unless directly and permissibly quoted

## Synthesis formula

- project / brand DNA: at least 50%
- historical or regional lineage grammar: normally 15–30%
- master-method references combined: no more than 35%
- experimental move: 5–15%

The final generation prompt omits names and preserves only translated design instructions.
