# Photorealism and AI Artifact QA

## People

Check hands, fingers, eyes, teeth, ears, hair edges, skin pore scale, clothing seams, body balance, gaze direction, repeated faces and over-sharpened skin.

## Products

Lock product proportions, cap and label geometry, typography, reflections, transparent-wall thickness, liquid level, refraction, droplets, contact shadow and scale relative to hands/table/environment.

## Food and hospitality

Check dish count, portion logic, table perspective, plate ellipses, ingredient scale, steam direction, utensil interaction, serving sequence and repeated food forms. Do not use random fruit or garnish as filler.

## Space and exhibition

Check verticals, vanishing points, door/furniture/person scale, floor-wall junctions, material seams, panel thickness, installation clearances, light sources, contact shadows and impossible floating structures.

## Image finish

Reject halo sharpening, plastic skin, uniform denoise, fake bokeh, mismatched grain, inconsistent shadow softness, excessive local contrast and surface texture that changes scale across the image.

Automated image-quality metrics may flag similarity or defects, but final realism and art direction require visual evidence and professional review.
