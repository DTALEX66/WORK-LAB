# Visual Jury Protocol

Use multiple roles because one generic “beauty score” hides disagreement.

## Jury roles

- brief and strategy reviewer
- composition and hierarchy reviewer
- typography reviewer
- image / photography reviewer
- color, material and lighting reviewer
- cultural-context reviewer
- originality and non-imitation reviewer
- production and handoff reviewer

## Decision method

1. score independently with visible evidence;
2. compare A/B before assigning an absolute score;
3. expose disagreement by axis;
4. require human calibration when disagreement exceeds 2 points, confidence is low, or the work depends on culturally sensitive interpretation;
5. preserve minority concerns even when the weighted result passes;
6. never let an automated perceptual metric override a hard production, cultural or originality failure.
