# Photography and Image Direction

A commercial image brief must define:

- subject relationship and action
- camera height, distance and lens feeling
- perspective and scale references
- key/fill/background light
- texture and retouching level
- crop and negative-space purpose
- product proportion and label fidelity
- scene plausibility and object count
- emotional temperature
- forbidden artifacts

Human and food scenes require special checks for anatomy, table perspective, repeated objects, false reflections, excessive sharpening and synthetic skin or food texture.
