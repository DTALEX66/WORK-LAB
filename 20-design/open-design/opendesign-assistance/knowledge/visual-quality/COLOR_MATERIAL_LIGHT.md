# Color, Material and Light

## Color

- assign semantic roles before choosing values
- control area ratio, not only palette swatches
- test lightness hierarchy and grayscale separation
- account for RGB/CMYK/gamut and substrate shifts
- reserve accent color for an intentional event

## Material

- specify surface, roughness, edge, thickness, join and wear logic
- avoid universal glossy plastic and impossible mirror behavior
- let material support brand meaning and production reality

## Light

- define key, fill, contrast and color-temperature logic
- shadows must agree on direction, softness and contact
- highlights follow geometry and roughness
- atmospheric light must not destroy information hierarchy
