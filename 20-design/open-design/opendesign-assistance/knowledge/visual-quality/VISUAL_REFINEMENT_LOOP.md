# Visual Refinement Loop

The loop is surgical, not a complete regeneration by default.

1. capture current artifact and score report
2. identify the three highest-impact weaknesses
3. select no more than three interventions
4. patch structure before surface
5. compare A/B at thumbnail, normal and detail views
6. record gains and regressions
7. stop when score ≥ 8.5 with no hard gate, gain < 0.2 for two rounds, or iteration = 4
8. request human calibration for culturally sensitive, highly original or subjective final selection

Preserve what already works. Every round must list `keep`, `change`, and `do not touch`.
