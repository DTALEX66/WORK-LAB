# Style Analysis Protocol

Style is not a palette, effect or famous name. Analyze it as an interacting system.

## Eight layers

1. historical / regional lineage
2. problem and social purpose
3. macrostructure and reading behavior
4. typography and language
5. color, image and symbol
6. material, process and production evidence
7. motion, sequence and spatial behavior
8. commercial adaptation and maintenance

## Style vector

Record relative tendencies rather than absolute labels:

- structure
- expressiveness
- restraint
- tactility
- motion
- cultural specificity
- commercial clarity
- experimentation

Vectors are routing heuristics, not objective history. They must be revised when project evidence contradicts the default.

## Synthesis

- one primary lineage controls the overall logic;
- no more than two supporting lineages;
- each support lineage may alter one major layer;
- project DNA remains the majority influence;
- final generation prompts contain no designer or lineage names;
- every direction includes an anti-cliché and forbidden-signature list.
