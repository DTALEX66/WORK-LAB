# Style Synthesis Rules

1. Start from the project thesis and audience effect.
2. Choose one primary lineage and at most two supporting lineages.
3. Extract methods from at least two projects when studying a master.
4. Translate names into anonymous craft directives.
5. Keep incompatible tensions explicit rather than averaging them away.
6. Produce three directions with different structural bets, not color swaps.
7. Lock the chosen direction before application expansion.
8. Maintain a forbidden-signature list for every reference.
9. Track which decision came from brief, lineage, production or experiment.
10. Reject a direction if changing the logo and colors would make it fit any brand.
