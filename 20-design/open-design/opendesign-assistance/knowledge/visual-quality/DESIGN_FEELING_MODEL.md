# Design Feeling Model

“有感觉” is treated as a controlled relationship between intention and sensory effect, not a vague compliment.

## Feeling axes

- tension ↔ calm
- dense ↔ spacious
- warm ↔ cool
- precise ↔ organic
- tactile ↔ immaterial
- monumental ↔ intimate
- direct ↔ mysterious
- restrained ↔ exuberant
- stable ↔ dynamic
- familiar ↔ novel

## Evaluation

For each intended feeling:

1. state the target and acceptable range;
2. cite which composition, type, color, image, material, light or motion decisions create it;
3. identify conflicts where one decision produces the opposite effect;
4. compare the thumbnail, normal-view and detail-view reading;
5. reject feeling claims that are supported only by adjectives or moodboard labels.

A high-quality design can be quiet, loud, minimal, ornate, rational or expressive. The requirement is that the effect is intentional, coherent and suitable for the brief.
