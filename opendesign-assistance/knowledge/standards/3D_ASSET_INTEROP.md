# 3D asset interoperability

- Use glTF/GLB for efficient delivery and web/runtime previews.
- Use USD where layered scene composition and multi-tool collaboration are required.
- Use MaterialX/OpenPBR for portable material definitions when supported.
- Record units, coordinate system, up axis, scale, transforms, cameras, lights, mesh statistics, UVs and texture color spaces.
- PBR assets distinguish base color, metallic, roughness, normal, occlusion, emission and transmission properties.
- Preview images are not dimensional authority; fabrication uses CAD/BIM/technical drawings.
- Validate assets with an official or pinned validator and retain the report.
