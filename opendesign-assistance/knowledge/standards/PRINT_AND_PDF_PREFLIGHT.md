# Print and PDF preflight baseline

A print artifact cannot pass on appearance alone.

## Required checks

- Final trim size, bleed, safe area and page boxes.
- Effective image resolution at placed size.
- Color spaces, ICC output intent, transparency blending space and spot colors.
- Embedded/subset fonts and licensing.
- Overprint, rich black, total ink coverage and white-overprint hazards where relevant.
- Dielines, cut/crease/varnish/foil layers and named spot colors for packaging.
- Missing links, hidden layers, annotations, interactive media and non-printable content.
- PDF/X profile requested by the printer; do not assume one profile fits offset, digital, sign/display and packaging.
- A printer/job-ticket approval remains mandatory for production.
