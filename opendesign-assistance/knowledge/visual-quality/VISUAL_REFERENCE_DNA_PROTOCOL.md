# Visual Reference DNA Protocol

A reference is evidence to analyze, not pixels to imitate.

## Macro DNA

- canvas and aspect ratio
- dominant focal object
- reading path and hierarchy
- grid, alignment, balance and controlled imbalance
- density, whitespace and rhythm
- image-to-type ratio
- repeated modules and deliberate exceptions

## Typography DNA

- role of type: label, voice, object, texture or navigation
- type category, width, weight, contrast and language support
- scale ratios, line length, leading and tracking
- alignment, wrapping and interaction with image

## Image / camera DNA

- subject staging and relationship
- camera height, lens feeling, crop and depth of field
- lighting direction, softness, contrast and color temperature
- texture, retouching, grain and sharpening
- realism cues: contact shadow, reflection, refraction and scale

## Color / material DNA

- color-area percentages rather than swatch lists
- neutral/accent relationship
- surface roughness, transparency, reflectivity and edge behavior
- print, fabrication or rendering evidence

## Output rule

Produce an anonymous DNA report, a forbidden-copy list and at least three ways the DNA must change for the new brief. Never trace or reproduce an iconic composition.
