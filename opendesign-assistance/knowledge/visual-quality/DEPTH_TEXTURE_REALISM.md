# Depth, Texture and Realism

Realism comes from consistent causes, not maximum detail.

- perspective lines share a plausible camera
- object scale is mutually consistent
- contact shadows anchor objects
- edge softness changes with depth
- reflection and refraction match material
- texture scale matches object scale
- imperfections are sparse and causal
- depth of field follows lens and focus distance
- print texture follows ink and substrate
- 3D scenes include seams, thickness, junctions and gravity

Reject “hyper-detailed” output when sharpness, microtexture and contrast exceed the physical scene.
