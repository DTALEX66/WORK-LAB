# Visual Quality Model V2.1.1

Visual quality is evaluated in four layers and twenty-four axes.

## A. Meaning and direction

1. brief fidelity
2. audience relevance
3. conceptual specificity
4. emotional intention
5. cultural specificity and responsibility
6. originality / non-imitation

## B. Structure and communication

7. focal hierarchy
8. composition and balance
9. reading path
10. rhythm and pacing
11. typography craft
12. language and script handling

## C. Sensory and visual craft

13. color logic and area ratio
14. image / photography direction
15. material credibility
16. lighting motivation
17. depth and spatial coherence
18. texture and surface control
19. micro-detail finishing

## D. System and delivery

20. system consistency
21. variation without drift
22. medium realism
23. cross-format coherence
24. production readiness

## Three evidence layers

- deterministic: dimensions, contrast, spacing, files, fonts, color space and production checks
- perceptual: hierarchy, rhythm, coherence, realism and before/after comparison
- professional judgment: concept, cultural reading, originality, emotion and market fit

Automated metrics are advisory. They cannot establish artistic quality by themselves. Hard failures override the weighted score.
