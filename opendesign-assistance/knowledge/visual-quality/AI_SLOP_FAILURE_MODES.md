# AI Visual Slop Failure Modes

## Structural
- interchangeable hero, three cards and CTA
- equal emphasis everywhere
- no project-specific object or visual thesis
- desktop squeezed into mobile

## Surface
- generic aurora gradients and floating glass
- gold-on-black used as automatic luxury
- identical rounded rectangles
- random grain, glow, chrome or holographic effects
- over-sharpened people, food and products

## Image
- warped text and labels
- repeated objects and hands
- inconsistent camera or shadows
- impossible material transitions
- fake depth made only with blur

## Cultural
- symbolic clichés without context
- copying a famous designer's signature motif
- decorative calligraphy detached from language

## Review rule
Name the failure, point to evidence, remove or repair the cause, then compare before and after.
