# Master-grade Finishing Protocol

Refinement is sequential. Surface polish cannot rescue a weak structure.

## Pass 0 — Remove false quality

Remove generic glow, glass, gradients, metallic effects, random grain, fake depth, excessive sharpening and decorative complexity that do not serve the concept.

## Pass 1 — Structural bet

Lock focal system, reading path, macro proportions, negative space, rhythm and application logic.

## Pass 2 — Typography

Correct hierarchy, line breaks, font roles, optical alignment, language pairing, number treatment and spacing.

## Pass 3 — Image direction

Correct subject staging, crop, lens, perspective, realism, visual metaphor, retouching and image-to-type relationship.

## Pass 4 — Color, material and light

Set color-area ratios, contrast hierarchy, surface behavior, contact shadows, reflection/refraction and lighting motivation.

## Pass 5 — Micro craft

Inspect edges, corners, baselines, icon stroke, masks, texture scale, transitions, component states, print joins and repeated details.

## Pass 6 — Cross-format and production

Check that the same DNA survives mobile, wide, square, print, spatial and motion outputs without becoming a color-swap template.

## Stop conditions

Stop when the score is at least 8.5, no hard gate fails, the last two rounds gain less than 0.2, or four bounded refinement rounds are complete. Preserve a keep/change/do-not-touch record every round.
