# Master / Studio Research Protocol V2.1

A designer name is a **research query**, never a rendering filter.

## Evidence gate

Before a method is used in commercial generation:

1. verify the person or studio identity and discipline;
2. use at least two credited sources, including one primary, institutional, award, archive, museum or studio source when available;
3. examine at least three works or phases for an anchor card and five for a new registry seed;
4. record commission context, audience, medium, production constraints and date/context where verified;
5. separate visible observation, documented statement and local inference;
6. record exceptions and evolution instead of flattening a career into one look;
7. list protected or iconic elements that must not be copied.

## Twelve analysis dimensions

- problem and commission context
- audience and cultural context
- medium and production constraints
- macrostructure and reading path
- typography and language handling
- color system and area ratios
- image, photography and illustration direction
- material, light and surface
- repetition, variation and system behavior
- micro-detail and finishing
- commercial translation across applications
- exceptions and changes across periods

## Required output

```text
verified identity and sources
observations with evidence
inferences with confidence
transferable methods
quality signals
exceptions
forbidden signature elements
project-specific translation
name-free generation directives
```

The registry may contain discovery seeds. A seed is not an authoritative method card until this protocol is completed.
