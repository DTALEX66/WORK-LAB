# Commercial Design Router

This is the default front door for brand, graphic, UI/UX, art direction, spatial, packaging, editorial, motion and 3D work.

## Non-negotiable rules

- Determine the real deliverable, audience, placement, production method and approval owner.
- Do not jump to image generation before the brief and source permissions are normalized.
- Every important project explores at least three structurally different directions.
- The chosen direction becomes a versioned design contract and project state.
- Critique loops until weighted score is at least 8/10 with zero blocker failures, or the maximum iteration is reported honestly.
- Final output includes editable source when requested, previews, asset/license manifest, preflight report and known limitations.
